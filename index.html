Frequently Asked Questions

How do I bulk encode or add many inventory items at once?

You can easily bulk encode using Excel. Simply open the app, go to the bottom of the side menu, and click "Export Backup" to download the .xlsx template. Open this file on your computer, fill in your items under the inventory sheet, and then click "Import Excel" in the app to upload your entire list instantly.

Do I need an internet connection to use stashRx?

No! stashRx is an offline-first application. You can log operations, manage inventory, and process computations with zero lag even if your pharmacy's WiFi goes down. Internet is only required for the initial load and for the Premium Silent Cloud Sync feature to back up your data.

What happens if my tablet breaks or I clear my browser cache?

If you are using the Free Version, your data is stored locally. You must manually export your Excel backups regularly; otherwise, clearing your cache will result in data loss. If you are on the Full License (Premium), the app features Silent Cloud Sync, which automatically backs up your journal to our secure servers every 5 minutes. Your data is always safe.

Do I need to install anything from the App Store or Google Play?

No installation is required. stashRx runs directly in your browser. For the best experience, simply open the app link in Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android) and tap "Add to Home Screen". It will look and act exactly like a native app.

Does it compute Senior Citizen and PWD discounts?

Yes. Both the Free and Premium versions include automated SC/PWD computations (20% discount + VAT exemption) and allow you to log the customer's Name and ID number for compliance.
