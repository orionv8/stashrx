FAQ

Common questions.

Is my data safe if it's stored offline?

+
Yes. Your data is stored in your browser's IndexedDB — a secure, sandboxed database on your device. No one can access it remotely. We also provide export/import backup so you can save copies to your files or cloud drive of choice.

Is stashRx a BIR-registered POS or CAS?

+
No. stashRx is designed strictly as a digital inventory and sales tracking aid. It is not a registered Computerized Accounting System (CAS) or Point of Sale (POS). Users should maintain their manual ledgers and official receipts as required by BIR.

Can I use stashRx on multiple devices?

+
The Free version works on any device with a browser. Premium licenses are device-locked for security — each device needs its own license. You can transfer data between devices using the export/import backup feature.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

+
Clearing browser data will erase your stashRx database. That's why we built one-tap backup export — regularly save a backup file to keep your data safe. You can restore it anytime with the import feature.

How does the SC/PWD discount calculation work?

+
stashRx automatically applies the legally mandated 20% discount for Senior Citizens and Persons with Disabilities on applicable items. Just toggle the SC/PWD option during checkout and the discount is computed instantly.
